We study macroscopic and kinetic models of traffic and transport, and the coupling conditions that connect them at the junctions of a network.

Current work includes a multi-class kinetic traffic model that describes interactions between vehicle classes through braking and relaxation terms. For kinetic models on networks, we derive macroscopic coupling conditions at the junctions from the kinetic ones through half-space problems.
