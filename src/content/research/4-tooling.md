We build tools that make parameter studies reproducible and their results easy to explore.

Sweeps are declared once and expanded into individual runs. Each result is cached under a hash of its parameters, so re-running a study only computes what is missing. The results can then be explored interactively, on a local machine or from a compute node through the browser.
