This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, and loads its fonts from this site rather than from a third party.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, your browser sends technical data such as your IP address to GitHub, which processes it to deliver and secure the site. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## External links

Links to GitHub, Zenodo, arXiv and other sites lead to services with their own privacy terms. No data is sent to them until you follow a link.
